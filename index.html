Boys' Brigade · Bristol

Setup

One per line, each with its coordinates (like 51.4545,-2.5879), a full Google Maps link, or an address or place name to look up. To label an address, put the label first with a colon, like Old Kent Road: Queen Square, Bristol. For a what3words address, tap Navigate in the what3words app and paste the address it gives instead. To get coordinates on a phone, long-press the spot in Google Maps to drop a pin, then copy them from the search box. Addresses are looked up with OpenStreetMap Nominatim, © OpenStreetMap contributors.
